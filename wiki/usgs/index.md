# USGS — US Geological Survey

US Department of the Interior agency providing geological, hydrological, biological, and geographic surveys. For energy work, the relevant corpora are oil-and-gas resource assessments, mineral economics, and earthquake hazard / seismicity data.

## Data corpus scope

- **Oil & gas resource assessments**: National Assessment of Oil and Gas, World Petroleum Resources Project, basin-level technically-recoverable resource estimates
- **Mineral economics**: Mineral Commodity Summaries (annual), Mineral Industry Surveys (monthly)
- **Earthquake hazard**: National Seismic Hazard Maps, ShakeMap, instrument station data
- **Hydrologic data**: National Water Information System (NWIS) — streamflow, water quality

No worldenergydata library wrapper exists yet for USGS data. Scrapers are TBD per [worldenergydata#429](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/worldenergydata/issues/429) follow-on.

## Public-domain status

Verified 2026-05-20: usgs.gov/information-policies states "USGS-authored or produced data and information are considered to be in the U.S. public domain" (17 USC §105). `last_license_check: 2026-05-20`.

**Carve-out**: USGS occasionally publishes data jointly with non-federal partners. Per-source verification required when a USGS page cites a co-author from a state geological survey, university, or industry consortium — those joint datasets may carry additional terms.
