# MMS — Minerals Management Service (legacy)

The Minerals Management Service was the US Department of the Interior agency responsible for offshore oil-and-gas leasing, royalty collection, and safety regulation on the Outer Continental Shelf from 1982 to 2010.

## Dissolution

Dissolved in 2010 in the wake of the Deepwater Horizon oil spill. Functions were split across three successor agencies:

- **BSEE** (Bureau of Safety and Environmental Enforcement) — safety enforcement, inspections, incident response
- **BOEM** (Bureau of Ocean Energy Management) — leasing, environmental review, resource assessment
- **ONRR** (Office of Natural Resources Revenue) — royalty collection, audit, compliance

For post-2010 material, route to the relevant successor agency (BSEE pages live at [`wiki/bsee/`](../bsee/index.md); BOEM and ONRR do not yet have wiki domains here — TBD).

## Data corpus scope (pre-2010)

Legacy MMS publications, technical reports, Notices to Lessees (NTLs), and Minerals Revenue Management reports retain their public-domain status under 17 USC §105. Specific corpus:

- Notices to Lessees and Operators (NTL series, all years through 2010)
- Outer Continental Shelf Reports (resource assessments, leasing summaries)
- Inspection and incident reports (transferred to BSEE 2011+)
- Royalty management reports (transferred to ONRR 2011+)

## Public-domain status

US federal government work, 17 USC §105. Pre-dissolution material retains federal-public-domain status regardless of dissolution. `last_license_check: 2026-05-20`.
